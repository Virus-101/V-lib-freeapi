Bugs the tests caught (all real, all fixed)

  • A 500 exhausting retries raised RateLimitError — sleeping on a permanent failure hides the cause. Split QUOTA_STATUS from RETRYABLE_STATUS.
  • _spec_key sat outside the per-provider try — one missing key killed the entire registry build. Now non-fatal.
  • The generic provider rejected an empty key even when the spec declared no auth — unusable for exactly the private-server case it is for.
  • list("hi") sent two one-character messages — the coercion belongs at the provider boundary, not only in the client.
  • requires_tools=False filtered out every tool-capable model for a plain text request.
  • A whitespace-only key was treated as present.
  • vl mid-name (qwen2.5-vl-72b) was not detected as vision.

The quota, handled as a domain signal

# anonymous tier, no referrer → HTTP 402
{"status":402,"error":{"code":"FREE_LIMIT",
 "message":"Free limit reached: 1 reply every 30 seconds per IP.
           Retry after 15s, or get unlimited replies…"}}

# freeaipi raises, carrying the provider's own delay:
RateLimitError(retry_after=15.0)   # parsed from the body

# and with a referrer (sent by default), the quota disappears entirely

A 500 that survives its retries becomes ProviderError, never RateLimitError — sleeping on a permanent failure hides the cause. Fallback never fires past a quota: dodging a rate limit by switching models hides the limit from the caller.
