The quota, handled as a domain signal

# anonymous tier, no referrer → HTTP 402
{"status":402,"error":{"code":"FREE_LIMIT",
 "message":"Free limit reached: 1 reply every 30 seconds per IP.
           Retry after 15s, or get unlimited replies…"}}

# freeaipi raises, carrying the provider's own delay:
RateLimitError(retry_after=15.0)   # parsed from the body

# and with a referrer (sent by default), the quota disappears entirely

A 500 that survives its retries becomes ProviderError, never RateLimitError — sleeping on a permanent failure hides the cause. Fallback never fires past a quota: dodging a rate limit by switching models hides the limit from the caller.
